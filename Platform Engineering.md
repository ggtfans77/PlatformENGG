# 🏗️ Platform Engineering Roadmap (2026 Edition)
**From Application Administrator to Platform Architect**

In 2026, the "successor" to DevOps is the shift toward **Platform Engineering**. This roadmap outlines the technical skills, mindset shifts, and certifications required to transition your career.

---

## 🛠️ Phase 1: Infrastructure as Code (IaC) & Automation
As an App Admin, you move from manual configuration to "Everything as Code."

*   **Provisioning:** Master [Terraform](www.terraform.io) or [OpenTofu](opentofu.org) to manage cloud infrastructure across AWS, Azure, or GCP.
*   **Scripting:** Move beyond basic Bash; learn **Go** (the language of the cloud) or **Python** for building custom automation tools.
*   **Version Control:** Everything must reside in Git. Proficiency in [GitHub Actions](github.com) or [GitLab CI](about.gitlab.com) is mandatory.

## ⛵ Phase 2: Cloud-Native Orchestration
Kubernetes is the "Operating System" of the 2026 data center.

*   **Kubernetes (K8s):** Learn to manage clusters, not just apps. Focus on **Custom Resource Definitions (CRDs)** and **Operators**.
*   **GitOps:** Adopt [Argo CD](argoproj.github.io) or [Flux](fluxcd.io) to ensure the live environment automatically stays in sync with your Git repository.
*   **Service Mesh:** Understand how microservices communicate using [Istio](istio.io) or [Linkerd](linkerd.io).

## 💡 Phase 3: The "Product" Mindset & DevEx
The core of Platform Engineering is treating the platform as a **product** where the Developers are your **customers**.

*   **Internal Developer Portals (IDP):** Learn to build portals using [Backstage.io](backstage.io) or [Port](www.getport.io). These allow developers to "self-serve" their own environments.
*   **Golden Paths:** Create standardized, "hardened" templates. Developers shouldn't need to know Kubernetes; they should just push code into a template you built.
*   **AIOps Integration:** Incorporate AI agents to handle "self-healing" infrastructure and predictive scaling.

## 🎓 Phase 4: 2026 Recommended Certifications
Validate your transition with these industry-recognized credentials:

1.  **CKA:** [Certified Kubernetes Administrator](training.linuxfoundation.org) (Fundamental).
2.  **CNPA:** **Certified Cloud Native Platform Engineering Associate** (The specific 2026 standard for this role).
3.  **HashiCorp Certified:** [Terraform Associate](www.hashicorp.com).
4.  **AWS Solutions Architect:** [Professional Level](aws.amazon.com).
